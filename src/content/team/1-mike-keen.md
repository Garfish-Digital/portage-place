Mike is president of The Bakery Group and managing partner of Hometowne
Development. He earned his PhD in sociology at Notre Dame and spent thirty years
teaching at Indiana University South Bend, where he founded the Center for a
Sustainable Future in 2008. In 2016 the State of Indiana named him a Sagamore of
the Wabash, its highest civilian honor.

He lives in the Near Northwest Neighborhood, which is the shortest explanation of
why this building got rescued. He is also the person who coined *gentle-fication*
for what the partners are trying to do here — recovery that benefits the people
already living on the block.
