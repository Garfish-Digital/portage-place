Tyler leads sustainability for Inovateus Solar and co-owns Logistick, both South
Bend companies. He studied business at Holy Cross College and returned to Grand
Valley State for an MBA in sustainability.

He sits on the boards of PVpallet and the St. Joseph County Parks Foundation, and
on a regional leadership council for the Michigan Sustainable Business Forum. He
lives in a solar-powered tiny house a few streets from here — which tells you most
of what you need to know about how seriously he takes the subject.
