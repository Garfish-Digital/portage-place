Dwayne grew up in his father's construction business — crews, a lumber yard, and a
truss plant started in 1962 — became president in 1995 and bought the company in
2011. Borkholder Buildings now supplies designs and material packages through more
than fifty dealers across the Midwest.

His newer venture, **New Energy Homes**, builds zero-energy houses at market rates,
including four with the local Habitat for Humanity chapter right here in the Near
Northwest and a line of net-zero tiny homes that debuted in the same neighborhood.
He has carried the same building technology into humanitarian projects abroad.
