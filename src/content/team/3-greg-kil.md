Greg founded Kil Architecture / Planning in 1991; the firm now employs eleven
people and has spent three decades working on buildings around this region. He
holds a master's in architecture and urban design from Notre Dame, taught there
and at Andrews University, and spent four seasons as staff architect on an
Etruscan excavation in Italy.

He is a Qualified Historic Architect with the Indiana Division of Historic
Preservation and Archaeology, which is the credential that matters most here — this
project is as much preservation as it is construction.
