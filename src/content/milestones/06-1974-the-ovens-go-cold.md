Ward's South Bend bakery closed on a Friday. **Eighty-five people** lost their
jobs.

General manager William Upton said the company hoped to reopen once engineers had
looked at what new equipment would cost. Tip-Top would keep reaching South Bend in
the meantime, trucked in from Chicago.

The bakery never reopened. What makes it land harder is that it was not a slow
death — as recently as 1972 Ward was announcing new distribution centers and
reporting sales at an all-time high.
