Ward wanted a **$38,000 garage** at 908 Portage. To get it, the company needed
the consent of three-quarters of the surrounding property owners — and the
neighbors were not enthusiastic about gasoline trucks firing up before dawn.

Ward's answer was to promise **electric delivery trucks**, quiet enough to run in
the early morning without waking the street.

It is a small detail, and it is the earliest evidence in this building's file that
whoever occupies it has to answer to the people who live around it. That has never
stopped being true.
