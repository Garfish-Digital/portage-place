The roof went. Water got in. Conditions inside got worse every winter, and asbestos
and lead paint turned a derelict building into an expensive problem. Mike Keen has
called it the "elephant in the room" for the Near Northwest — a structure that
frightened people who did not live there and pulled down the value of every house
around it.

It was not for lack of trying. **Near Northwest Neighborhood, Inc.** applied for
Low-Income Housing Tax Credits **six separate times** to convert the building into
affordable housing. The best-documented attempt, in 2013, would have delivered 42
apartments and ground-floor retail for around $8 million.

All six applications were denied. Developer after developer toured the building and
came to the same conclusion: there was no economical way to do this.
