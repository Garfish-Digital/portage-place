Ward spent roughly **$500,000** modernizing the Portage plant. The new machinery
could turn out about **4,000 loaves an hour**, carrying a loaf from mixing bowl to
wrapper with almost no human hands on it. A major new oven went in as part of the
work.

On **16 October 1955**, the bakery exploded.

Damage came to around $5,000 and soot coated a good portion of the building. Nobody
was hurt. The open house scheduled to show off the new equipment was quietly
cancelled.
