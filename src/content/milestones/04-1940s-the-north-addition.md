The second major construction phase pushed the building north, toward the corner
at California Avenue, and turned a single-purpose plant into the multi-period
complex that stands today.

South Bend's own **Thos. L. Hickey, Inc.** is the only builder ever named for any
phase of this structure, according to the Hickey family's history — which also
holds a photograph of the finished addition.

The result is that no single date describes this building. Parts of it are older
than the bakery, the bakery is 1919, and the north end is a generation younger
again.
